Our services

Mpilo Central Hospital is a public referral hospital serving Bulawayo and the surrounding provinces. These are the departments you can book through MAHMS.

Outpatient

General consultations and the walk-in clinic. Start here if you are not sure which department you need.

Cardiology

Heart and circulatory care, including blood pressure review and follow-up after a cardiac event.

Maternity

Antenatal visits, delivery and postnatal care.

Paediatrics

Care for children under 13, including routine vaccinations.

Orthopaedics

Bones, joints and trauma, including fractures and sports injuries.

Dermatology

Skin conditions, rashes and allergies.

Neurology

Brain and nervous system, including migraine and seizure management.

Mental Health

Psychiatry and counselling.

Radiology

X-ray, ultrasound and other imaging, by referral.

Pharmacy

Dispensary for prescriptions written at the hospital.

Consultation fees

Type of consultationFee (USD)
General practitioner, paediatrics20.00
Specialist (cardiology, neurology, dermatology, psychiatry)30.00
Surgery, radiology40.00

Fees are payable by EcoCash, card or cash. You get a numbered receipt either way. Emergency treatment is never refused for inability to pay.

How booking works

  1. Sign in with your patient account, or register in the mobile app.
  2. Choose the department and the doctor you want to see.
  3. Pick a date and time, and say briefly why you are coming.
  4. Your appointment starts as Pending and becomes Confirmed once the department accepts it.
  5. Pay on the day, or in advance through the app.
