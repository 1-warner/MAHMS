Healthcare appointments without the queue

MAHMS is the appointment and healthcare management system for Mpilo Central Hospital in Bulawayo. Book a doctor, keep track of your prescriptions, pay for a visit and reach the casualty desk in an emergency, from your phone or this website.

Sign in to your account

What you can do

Book an appointment

Choose a department and a doctor, pick a time that suits you, and get a confirmation. No standing in line to be given a slot.

See your prescriptions

Every prescription your doctor writes appears in your account, with the dosage and how long to take it. Set a daily reminder in the mobile app.

Pay and get a receipt

Settle a consultation fee by EcoCash, card or cash at the desk, and keep the numbered receipt. You can save it as a PDF.

Emergency SOS

In the mobile app, one button alerts the casualty desk and sends your location, so help knows where to find you.

Get the mobile app

The full MAHMS experience, including the emergency SOS button and medication reminders, is in the Android app. This website covers appointments and the information pages.

The app is distributed by the hospital. Ask at reception, or contact us.

In an emergency

Do not use this website to report an emergency. Call the hospital directly on +263 29 221 7111, or use the SOS button in the MAHMS mobile app, which alerts the casualty desk and sends your location.
